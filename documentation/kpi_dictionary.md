# KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | SUM of net sales amount |
| Total Profit | SUM of profit |
| Profit Margin | Total Profit / Total Revenue |
| Total Orders | Count of unique orders |
| Total Customers | Count of unique customers with activity |
| Average Order Value | Revenue / unique orders |
| Return Rate | Returned orders / total orders |
| YoY Growth | (Current Year Revenue - Prior Year Revenue) / Prior Year Revenue |
| MoM Growth | (Current Month Revenue - Prior Month Revenue) / Prior Month Revenue |
| Customer Contribution | Customer revenue / total revenue |
| Product Rank | Revenue rank within category |
| Store Rank | Profit rank within region |
