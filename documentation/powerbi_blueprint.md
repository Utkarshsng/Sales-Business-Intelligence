# Power BI Dashboard Blueprint

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
KPI cards: Revenue, Profit, Margin, Orders, Customers, AOV, YoY Growth.
Visuals: Revenue trend, regional revenue, category profit, top products.
Slicers: Year, Region, Channel, Category.

## Page 2 — Sales & Product Intelligence
Visuals: Category revenue/profit, product ranking, discount vs margin,
return rate by category/product, top and bottom products.
Slicers: Category, Subcategory, Brand, Region.

## Page 3 — Customer Intelligence
Visuals: New vs returning customers, segment revenue, purchase frequency,
customer contribution, top customers, AOV by segment.
Slicers: Segment, Region, Year.

## Page 4 — Regional & Operational Analysis
Visuals: Store ranking, regional performance, returns, payment mix,
shipping cost, order status.
Slicers: Region, Store, Channel, Payment Method.
